JobMatch Ghana: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

JobMatch Ghana is a WhatsApp chatbot that helps informal-sector workers in Greater Accra find jobs that fit their skills. It is a research pilot, run as part of a postgraduate (MSc) research project. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp number, which WhatsApp shares with us when you message us.
  • Your work profile, only after you agree: your trade, skills, years of experience, when you can work and the areas (up to three districts) where you can work.
  • The messages you send to the bot and the bot's replies.
  • Your job matches and choices: which jobs we showed you, whether you said you were interested, and any rating or comment you gave.
  • Your CV details, only if you ask for a free CV and agree: the name to put on it, the phone number you choose to show (or none), past jobs, training, school and languages.

We do not ask for your ID card, date of birth, home address or photos, and we do not read anything outside your chat with JobMatch Ghana. Without a CV we do not ask for your name.

Consent

Before saving any work details, the bot asks whether you agree. If you say no, no profile is created. You can stop or delete your data at any time (see below).

How we use it

  • Job matching: to find suitable jobs in or near your areas and send them to you.
  • Connecting you to employers: when you tap I'm interested, the JobMatch team contacts you to arrange the connection. Your number is not posted publicly or given to employers without that step.
  • Your CV: your CV details are used only to make your CV, which we send to you in the chat as a PDF. We do not share your CV with anyone; you decide who to send it to.
  • Research: to measure how well the chatbot understands and matches people. Research data is pseudonymised (your number is replaced by a code) and excludes the text of your messages. Published results contain only totals, never individuals.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the messages between you and us, under WhatsApp's privacy policy.
  • Your data is stored on a secured server run by the JobMatch Ghana project. Only the project team can sign in to see it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as the pilot runs, or until you delete it. An unfinished chat is kept for 72 hours so you can continue where you stopped. When the pilot ends, personal data is deleted; only the pseudonymised research data is kept for the research report.

Delete your data

Send delete to JobMatch Ghana on WhatsApp and confirm. This removes your profile, skills, job matches, CV details, every message you sent us and your chat session, straight away.

Other commands: stop stops all messages from us; update lets you change your profile; help explains the options.

If you can no longer use WhatsApp, email us (below) from any address with the WhatsApp number you used, and we will delete the data for you.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you may ask to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Contact us below.
