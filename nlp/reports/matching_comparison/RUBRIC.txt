Score each row in the 'relevance' column, judging only the worker's trade and skills against the job:
  2 = relevant: the worker could do this job with the skills listed
  1 = partly relevant: same or neighbouring trade, but the job mainly needs skills the worker did not list
  0 = not relevant: different trade, or the skills do not fit
Ignore location, pay and availability. Do not open the key file until every row is scored.
A second rater scores about a third of the rows in 'relevance_rater2' without seeing your scores.

Decision rule agreed by rater 1 before scoring (apply it to every row; give it to rater 2 as well):
  2 = the job's main skill (job_skills) is one the worker listed, or a clear near-synonym of it
  1 = same or neighbouring trade, but the job's main skill is a different one
  0 = different work, or the skills do not fit
Check job_skills against worker_skills on every row; a worker's other listed skills never count against them.
